## How to use resources

1. Pick one primary resource per topic; don't collect courses instead of building.
2. Keep your notes in your own words and link to the source.
3. Do not copy whole tutorials into this repository. Attribute snippets and follow each source's license/terms.
4. When a link is broken, search the exact title on the creator's official channel/site and update this file.


## Resource verification

Treat links as candidates until you open the exact lesson. Record the URL, access date, whether the lesson played, and any access/pricing limitation. The Medium page was intermittently inaccessible to automated fetching during setup; compare resource details with the article directly before assuming every item is unchanged. If a link fails, find an official replacement and document the substitution. Do not mark a video watched merely because its landing page opened.
